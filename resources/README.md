# Original Zelda title screen textures

`title_screen.png` is an unmodified, 256 × 224 pixel capture of the original NES *The Legend of Zelda* title screen, downloaded from [StrategyWiki](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/File:LOZ_NES_title_screen.png).

`title_background.png` removes the animated water and the “PUSH START BUTTON” text from that capture. `title_prompt.png` holds the text. The five `waterfall_*.png` textures are the original two crest frames and three wave frames, cropped from [The Spriters Resource title-screen sheet](https://www.spriters-resource.com/nes/legendofzelda/asset/32134/) and recolored to the source capture's NES palette. Compositing the matching sprite positions over the background reproduces `title_screen.png` pixel for pixel.

`triforce_glow_mask.png` covers the full triangle: the bar above ZELDA, the area behind its letters, and the lower tip. `triforce_occlusion.png` is drawn over it to keep the letters, vines, sword, and black outline in front. Both 72 × 72 textures are drawn at `(92, 48)`. The program colors the mask using the eight-step NES palette cycle `$27, $37, $37, $27, $17, $07, $07, $17` in [Aldo Núñez's Zelda disassembly](https://github.com/aldonunez/zelda1-disassembly/blob/master/src/Z_02.asm). The RGB values follow the [FCEUX palette](https://lospec.com/palette-list/fceux) used by the still capture. The disassembly also supplies the waterfall positions, movement, crest frame switch, and glow timing. The textures display at 3× size with nearest-neighbor filtering.

The artwork and game are © Nintendo. NES colors can vary with the console palette and display; these files preserve the source capture’s palette without recoloring.

`title_music.nsf` is the original NES cartridge music data from [Zophar's Domain's The Legend of Zelda NSF rip](https://www.zophar.net/music/nintendo-nes-nsf/legend-of-zelda-the). Track 1 is the title BGM. The game plays it continuously through [Game Music Emu](https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu), which emulates the NES sound hardware; its vendored source and LGPL license are in `vendor/game-music-emu/`. This uses the NES cartridge arrangement, which differs from the Famicom Disk System soundtrack. The music is © Nintendo and Koji Kondo.
